Read this first: five places the brief needs correcting

"Unicorn level"A valuation comes from distribution and data loops, not from how polished the product is. CastlerX has raised about $7.5M and has about 55 people (PitchBook). They can be beaten, but not by adding features. Excro has to win on its existing bank-connected client base, the viral loop where every counterparty is a new user, and outcome data no one else has. The plan is built around those three.
Azure-firstAzure is fine. The catch: the strongest models aren't available in-region. Claude on Microsoft Foundry is offered as Global Standard (plus a US data zone). Microsoft says in-UAE processing for GPT-4o/4.1 in UAE North needs Provisioned deployments. So documents are OCR'd and redacted inside the country, and payment data never goes to a model. Choose models by eval score, not by which ones the credits cover.
Reusing old agreementsClient agreements are confidential. Drafting from a shared pool of them without consent is a breach waiting to happen. Each tenant drafts from its own private library. The shared library holds only lawyer-drafted clauses plus opted-in, anonymised clauses. Every generated agreement must re-extract to exactly the same Release Schedule, or it's rejected.
Middle East real estateOff-plan escrow is already mandatory and run by banks (Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007; KSA Wafi). That isn't a gap to fill. The gaps are resale deposits (the ~10% Form F/MOU deposit often held by brokers), cross-border buyers, and India↔UAE trade escrow as a cheaper alternative to letters of credit.
Scope vs teamTwo people can't launch India, the UAE and every use case at once, or run 24×7 money operations. Plan: India goods-sale first, one bank, and Excro owns operations. UAE starts only after India hits its exit criteria.

Trust & accuracy: human in the loop by design

Accuracy doesn't come from trusting one model. It comes from redundancy, verifiable citations, and people confirming every condition.

1 · In-country OCR
Layout + textDocument Intelligence in the deal's own jurisdiction; page and position for every word
2 · Redact
Tokenise PIINames, IDs and accounts replaced with tokens before any model call
3 · Two readers
Independent extractionTwo model families extract into the same strict schema
4 · Verify
Quote must existEvery field cites text that is checked word-for-word against the OCR. No quote, no condition.
5 · Humans
Both parties tick every conditionNo "accept all". An edit by one side re-opens it for the other. Excro reviews high-value or contested deals.
ConfidenceRuleWhat the user sees
GreenBoth readers agree, citation verified, type checks passPre-filled; still needs each party's tick
AmberOnly one reader found it, or poor scan quality at the cited textMust open the source snippet before ticking
RedReaders disagree, citation fails, or clauses conflictBlocks signing until resolved; Excro reviewer required

Accuracy you can prove: a golden set of 300 double-annotated agreements. CI blocks any prompt or model change if condition recall drops below 95% or amount/date precision below 99%. In production, 5% of confirmed deals are re-audited every week.

Two ways a deal starts

Before either flow: the initiator sets how many parties (2–10) and their roles: payer, payee, broker/fee payee, verifier or observer. Every party must have an Excro account and Excro-verified KYC. Individuals: PAN, Aadhaar (offline via DigiLocker, never stored in full), mobile, email and bank. Entities: PAN, GSTIN, MCA record, documents, the signatory's own KYC and authority, and beneficial owners at 10% or more. No one signs and no escrow account opens until every party is verified.

After signing, both flows are identical: escrow account → funding → conditions → release. Excro doesn't do a legal review in either flow.

Why Flow A still needs a signature: the accepted fixes change how money moves. If they're only in-app clicks, the original signed agreement wins in any dispute, and the bank won't act without signed authority. A 2-page addendum e-signed through Protean closes that hole without re-reviewing the original.

Agreement intelligence: drafting from past agreements

  • Two libraries. Private (the tenant's own agreements, drafting only for that tenant) and shared (lawyer-drafted clauses plus opted-in, anonymised clauses). Tenant separation is enforced by search-index filters, not by instructions in a prompt.
  • Draft flow. Guided intake → best template → top clause variants ranked by fit and by outcome score → AI assembles the draft and fills deal facts → consistency check → round-trip test (the draft is re-extracted and must produce the same Release Schedule) → redline review by the parties.
  • Legal line. Every generated draft says it was drafted with AI assistance and should be reviewed with a lawyer. Optional paid review through Excro's legal partner. Clause wording changes go to legal review.

Onboarding & customisation

Counterparty in under 3 minutes: WhatsApp link → OTP → see the deal and conditions before KYC → tick conditions → KYC only when signing (PAN and GSTIN autofill, penny-drop, Aadhaar eSign; UAE Pass in the UAE). No app install.

Tenants customise through configuration, never custom code: brand and domain, allowed deal types and locked clauses, internal approval chains and limits, fee split and reseller markup, webhooks, ERP export and SSO. If a request needs code, it goes into the product for everyone or it doesn't get built. That keeps a 2-person team shipping.

Visibility built into the product: a "Verified by Excro" escrow-backed offer badge sellers can share, read-only status links for lenders and logistics partners, and SEO template-gallery pages generated from the anonymised clause library.

Azure architecture

Each jurisdiction is an isolated cell. A deal is pinned to one cell for life. Only redacted text leaves a cell, and only through the AI gateway.

Middle East entry

  • Regulation: escrow agents in the UAE must be regulated (CBUAE, DFSA or FSRA). TrustIn runs under ADGM FSRA Category 3C with Emirates NBD holding funds. Recommendation: run as the technology platform for a licensed agent or bank first. Consider Excro's own ADGM licence only once volume pays for the capital and compliance cost.
  • Lead use cases: India→UAE export escrow (Excro already serves the Indian exporter), UAE→India import advances, Dubai resale deposit escrow, and Indian buyers purchasing Dubai property.
  • Product changes: AED/USD/INR ledger with FX from the partner bank, a playbook that follows Incoterms (FOB vs CIF changes who bears transit loss), bill of lading and container-tracking evidence, UAE Pass, Arabic RTL, no interest on escrow balances.
  • KSA: after the UAE works, with a licensed Saudi partner and the Saudi Arabia East region.
