SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Supply Agreement ("Agreement") is made on 12 September 2026 between Nirmal Traders, a proprietorship having its place of business at Plot 22, Focal Point, Ludhiana, Punjab (the "Buyer"), and Kavya Electronics Private Limited, a company having its registered office at Peenya Industrial Area, Bengaluru, Karnataka (the "Seller").

1. GOODS
1.1 The Seller shall supply 40 units of refurbished business laptops (Grade A, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD) (the "Goods").

2. PRICE
2.1 The price per unit is Rs. 46,000/-.
2.2 The total consideration is Rs. 18,40,000/- (Rupees Eighteen Lakh Forty Thousand only).

3. PAYMENT THROUGH ESCROW
3.1 The Buyer shall deposit the total consideration into an escrow account within 3 days of execution of this Agreement.
3.2 The amount held in escrow shall be released to the Seller upon acceptance of the Goods by the Buyer.

4. DELIVERY
4.1 The Seller shall dispatch the Goods by courier within 5 days of the escrow deposit being confirmed.
4.2 Delivery shall be made at Plot 22, Focal Point, Ludhiana 141003 to Nirmal Traders on or before 30 September 2026.
4.3 Seller's premises: Peenya Industrial Area, Bengaluru 560058.

5. INSPECTION
5.1 The Buyer shall inspect the Goods within 3 days of delivery and notify the Seller of acceptance or rejection.

6. LATE DELIVERY
6.1 If the Goods are delivered after the date in clause 4.2, the Seller shall pay liquidated damages of 0.5% of the value of the Goods per day of delay.

7. PAYMENT TERMS
7.1 Any balance amount payable under this Agreement shall be paid within 30 days of invoice.

8. GOVERNING LAW
8.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of India and the courts at Bengaluru shall have exclusive jurisdiction.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have signed this Agreement on the date first written above.
