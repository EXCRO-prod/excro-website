# 11 · Middle East expansion

## What the research says (Sep 2026)
- **Escrow is regulated in the UAE.** Escrow agents need CBUAE licensing or a DFSA (DIFC) / FSRA (ADGM) regulatory permission. TrustIn operates under ADGM FSRA Category 3C ("providing money services") with client funds held at Emirates NBD. Titanium Escrow (with Apex Group) launched in 2021 as an ADGM FSRA-supervised escrow provider.
- **Off-plan real estate escrow is already mandated and bank-run.** Dubai Law No. 8 of 2007 requires developers to use project escrow accounts with approved banks. In KSA, off-plan sales run through REGA's Wafi programme, with SAMA rules for escrow accounts. Neither is a gap for Excro.
- **Real opportunity in real estate is outside the developer escrow:** resale deposits under the Dubai MOU (commonly a ~10% deposit held by the brokerage until transfer), cross-border buyers (Indian buyers are a large share of Dubai purchasers) who need a neutral holder before transfer at the trustee office, and brokers who don't want to hold client money.
- **India–UAE trade corridor:** CEPA-driven bilateral trade has passed US$100 bn; RBI and CBUAE have frameworks for rupee–dirham settlement. Letters of credit are costly and slow for SME-sized shipments (e.g. $15k–$250k), which is where digital escrow competes.
- **Azure:** UAE North/UAE Central are live; Microsoft has announced **Saudi Arabia East** for November 2026.

| Cross-border property purchase (Indian buyer) | Indian resident/NRI buyer, UAE seller | funds via LRS/NRE → UAE escrow → transfer → release | Excro's Indian distribution + UAE execution |

## Entry model (recommended)
1. **Partner, don't license, first.** Operate as the technology platform for a licensed UAE escrow agent or bank (ADGM/DIFC-regulated agent or a CBUAE bank). They hold funds and are the regulated party; Excro provides the platform and Indian-side distribution. Competitors like TrustIn are also potential partners for India-origin flows.
2. **Entity:** UAE free-zone tech entity (e.g. ADGM or DIFC Innovation licence) to contract with partners and hold Cell AE.
3. **Own licence later (ADGM FSRA money services)** only after volume justifies the capital, compliance staff and audit cost.
4. **Cross-border compliance:** Indian leg always through an AD Category-I bank (FEMA, export proceeds realisation/EDPMS reporting); UAE leg through the partner. No netting across borders by Excro.

## Product changes for the Middle East
Multi-currency (AED, USD, INR) ledger with FX from the partner bank (Excro never sets rates); Incoterms-aware playbook; trade-document evidence adapters; UAE Pass identity and signing; Arabic/RTL UI; Islamic-finance-friendly terms (no interest on escrow balances); DIFC/ADGM or UAE onshore arbitration choices in the dispute clause.
